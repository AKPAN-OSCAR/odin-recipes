A delicious and nutritious soup made with afang leafs and other ingredients.

Afang soup is a rich, nutrient-dense delicacy native to the Efik , Ibibio and Anaang people of southern Nigeria. It is prepared using a perfect combination of wild Afang leaves and water-leaf. The unique texture comes from slicing the Afang leaves very thinly and pounding them before adding them to the pot.

Steps to Prepare Afang Soup

  1. Get all the above ingredients ready
  2. Wash the ingredients the ingredients, that's the meat, perewinle, afang leaves, water-leaf, properly
  3. Steam the meats in separate pot
  4. Make sure the afang and water-leaf are all sliced separately into different trays
  5. Add normal quantity of water to the pot with the stockfish inside and set it on fire for some minutes till it boils
  6. After the water and stockfish inside gets boiling for long
  7. Add the sliced water-leaf inside the boiling water and leave it for some minutes
  8. Add some amount of salt, crayfish and spices to taste
  9. Add some amount of palmoil
  10. Add all steam meats to the pot
  11. Observe the quantity of water and make sure the soup is not getting dried out
  12. Wait for some minutes for proper cooking
  13. have a taste of the soup while on fire
  14. after verification of the taste close the pot for some 1.5 minutes
  15. Add the sliced affang leafs to the pot for 1 minutes
  16. Stir the soup well for 1 minutes
  17. Remove the pot from fire immediately after the one minutes of stiring
  18. food is ready
