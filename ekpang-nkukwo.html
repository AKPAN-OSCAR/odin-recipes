A delicious and nutritious soup made with water yam, coco yam and other ingredients.

Ekpang Nkukwo is also among the rich, nutrient-dense delicacy native to the Efik , Ibibio and Anaang people of southern Nigeria. It is prepared using a perfect combination of wild Afang leaves and water-leaf. The unique texture comes from slicing the Afang leaves very thinly and pounding them before adding them to the pot.

Steps to Prepare Ekpang Nkukwo

  1. Get all the ingredients setup
  2. peel some water yams and grind them
  3. peel some coco yams and grind them
  4. mix and turn the grinded yams with some amount of salts inside a tray or large bowl
  5. thoroughly watch the potatoes leafs and avoid wounding them
  6. wash the cocoyam leafs to remove any dirt or debris
  7. wash the perewinlkes
  8. get a large pot and pour some oil in the empty clean pot
  9. add the perewinkles to the oil in the empty pot
  10. mix the large quantiies of poatatoes leaf and cocoyam leaf in one bowl
  11. bring ur bowl of grinded yams and bowl of leafs and pot together
  12. sit tight and get ready for the work
  13. take each of the leafs, add two pinces of the grinded yams into the leaf and wrap it
  14. after each wraps, put them in that same pot in a well arranged manner
  15. do the step 14 until all the wraps are done
  16. boil water in a kertle till boilling point
  17. pour the boiling water into the pot of wraps
  18. now put the pot on fire for some minutes so the wraps can cook for 20mintes
  19. dont stirl or mix immediately after pouring the hot water in the pot while its on fire
  20. after 20 minutes, check if the wraps are cooked to your liking
  21. add the ingredients, meats, spices, more perewinkles, palmoils
  22. add more water if needed
  23. allow to cook and then stir after another 10 minutes
  24. have a taste and verify its okay
  25. ur meal is ready to be served, so put the pot out of fire
